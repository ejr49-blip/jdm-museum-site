Presentation overview

90s JDM Museum

This project turns the history of 1990s Japanese performance cars into a museum-style website focused on engineering innovation, motorsport influence, tuning culture, and long-term industry change.

Core idea

Main thesis

These cars mattered because they combined speed with durability, making performance feel attainable and trustworthy.

They also became global symbols through media, games, film, and enthusiast communities, which gave them cultural power far beyond Japan.

Artifact focus

Featured vehicles

  • Toyota Supra A80
  • Nissan Skyline GT-R R34
  • Mazda RX-7 FD
  • Honda NSX
  • Subaru Impreza WRX
  • Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution
  • Nissan 300ZX
  • Toyota AE86

Why it stands out

Design approach

Editorial museum look using a warm palette, serif display typography, glass-like panels, and large photographic treatments.

Content is organized as exhibit labels, curatorial essays, timelines, and artifact entries instead of a generic school-project layout.

Historical content

Automotive industry impact

  • Made reliability part of the performance conversation
  • Elevated tuning potential into a marketable value
  • Legitimized advanced AWD and electronics
  • Made Japanese design and engineering aspirational on a global scale

Build summary

What changed in the site

  • Rewrote the homepage and archive for clearer museum-style information architecture
  • Introduced a stronger visual system and responsive layout
  • Expanded the writing so each section explains why the cars mattered historically
  • Used higher-resolution car images with source attribution and lightbox viewing
